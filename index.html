👋About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Automation at the College of Engineering, Ocean University of China (B.Eng. expected June 2027). My research lies at the intersection of robot learning and tactile sensing: I am currently a research intern at the Agentic Intelligence Lab, Department of Data and Systems Engineering, The University of Hong Kong, where I work on tactile-glove software, synchronised multimodal data-collection pipelines, and the training and real-robot deployment of ACT and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies.

In parallel, I work on the control of autonomous marine and underwater systems — underwater gliders, remotely operated vehicles, and bio-inspired underwater robots — with a focus on disturbance-observer-based and prescribed-performance control under actuator saturation and unknown disturbances.

I am applying to the MSc(Eng) in Robotics and Intelligent Systems at HKU, with the goal of building embodied systems that can perceive contact and act reliably in unstructured environments.

TacEnvelope: A Plug-in Framework for Utilizing Tactile Envelope Feedback in RL Post-Training of VLA Policies

Huanzhang Hu, Bo Chen, Fangdi Yu, Haoyi Zhang, Xianxin Lai, Jiahui Chen, Haijier Chen, Xiaoquan Sun, Dongchen Zheng, Yijun Hong, Zurui Chen, Wenqi Ge†, and Jiayu Chen†

Under ReviewIEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) 2027 · Third author

Paper. A plug-in framework that learns adaptive tactile force envelopes from successful demonstrations and converts tactile deviations into dense reward signals for online RL post-training of VLA policies on contact-rich manipulation. Results. On real-world tasks, DSRL USB-insertion success rose from 87.5% to 95% with time-to-success reduced from 20 s to 13 s.

TACROSS: An Efficient and Low-Cost Scalable Human Touch System Across Heterogeneous Tactile Sensors for Dexterous Robot Learning

Bo Chen*, Huanzhang Hu*, Junyang Ma, Bo Yue, Fangdi Yu, Haijier Chen, Xianxin Lai, Shuyu Pan, Zhen Yang, Xiaoquan Sun, Wenze Cui, Zhongliang Jiang, Shaopeng Liu†, and Jiayu Chen†

Under ReviewarXiv:2610.11945; IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) 2027 · Fifth author

Paper. A scalable, low-cost human-touch data-collection system that aligns heterogeneous tactile sensors — piezoresistive human gloves and capacitive robot fingertips — into a shared contact-semantic space. It reduces data-collection cost by 95.7% and increases collection speed by 3.5×, releasing an open dataset of over 150 hours of human tactile demonstrations.

Other Publications

  • Extended Disturbance Observer Enhanced Quasi-Infinite-Horizon Nonlinear Model Predictive Control for Underwater Gliders

    Jiahui Lv, Fangdi Yu, and Tongfu He†

    PublishedProceedings of the 5th International Conference on Autonomous Unmanned Systems (ICAUS 2025), Lecture Notes in Electrical Engineering, vol. 1574, Springer, 2026, pp. 318–328 · Second author (of three) · DOI

    An extended disturbance observer (EDO) enhanced quasi-infinite-horizon NMPC scheme for underwater glider pitch motion, in which the observer estimates lumped disturbances online and compensates for them inside the predictive controller, improving tracking accuracy and disturbance rejection over standard NMPC.

  • Super-Twisting Sliding Mode Control of ROV Systems with Prescribed Performance under Actuator Saturation and Unknown Disturbances

    Tongfu He, Shiyu Wang, Dalei Song, Weicheng Sun, Qianli Jiang, and Fangdi Yu

    Published2025 40th Youth Academic Annual Conference of Chinese Association of Automation (YAC), Zhengzhou, China, 2025 · Sixth author · DOI

    A composite ROV controller integrating super-twisting sliding mode control (STSMC) with prescribed performance control (PPC), an extended state observer (ESO) for online disturbance estimation, and an anti-saturation auxiliary system. Simulations show reduced chattering, faster convergence, and improved tracking precision over conventional methods.
